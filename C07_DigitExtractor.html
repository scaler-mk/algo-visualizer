Why % 10 gives the last digit

Every base-10 number is the last digit plus 10× (everything-to-the-left). So when you divide by 10, the remainder IS the last digit. 2748 % 10 = 8 always.

Why / 10 drops the last digit

Integer division throws away the remainder. 2748 / 10 = 274 — the 8 is gone. Combine the two and you can walk every digit, right to left, until nothing is left.
