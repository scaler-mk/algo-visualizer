Iterables and iterators — the playlist and the music player

An iterable is a collection of items (the playlist). An iterator is what walks through it, remembering where it is (the music player). The for loop creates the player for you.

UNDER THE HOOD — for-loop sugar

What you write

for item in playlist: print(item)

What Python actually does

# 1. Get an iterator it = iter(playlist) while True: try: item = next(it) except StopIteration: break print(item)
Press next ▸ to advance the iterator. Each press is one call to next(it). The iterator remembers where it left off between calls — that's its only job.
