Floor division vs int() — they're NOT the same

Both // and int() give you whole numbers — but they round in different directions for negatives. Try a few values, especially negative ones. The number line makes it click.

The key insight
For positive numbers, they agree. For negative numbers, they differ: int() moves towards zero, while // moves towards negative infinity. Quiz 6 hinges on this: -8 // 3 = -3 (not -2), because the largest integer ≤ -2.666… is -3.
