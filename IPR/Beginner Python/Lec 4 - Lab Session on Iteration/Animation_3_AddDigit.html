Add a digit to the back — n × 10 + d

Multiply n by 10 to make room for a zero at the back, then add d to fill it. Try your own numbers.
N (the number)
d (single digit, 0-9)
QUICK EXAMPLES
THE FORMULA
new = 42×10 + 7
= 420 + 7 = 427
STEP BY STEP
1
Start with 42
2
Multiply by 10 → 42 × 10 = 420. A 0 appears at the back, ready to be replaced.
3
Add 7 → 420 + 7 = 427. The new digit takes its place.
Why × 10? In base-10, every digit shifts left when you multiply by 10 — leaving a fresh 0 on the right. Then + d simply replaces that 0 with the digit you want.

AFTER + d — digit slotted in
