Hold on… did you catch that?

Same list. Same word. Two different answers.

AI silently switched its definition of "middle" between the two questions — and didn't tell you.

Question 1 — "Find the middle"

25
AI used the average of the two middle values (20 and 30).

Question 2 — "Middle × 10"

200
AI picked one middle value (20) and multiplied it.

Why? Because with an even-sized list, there's no single middle — there are two. AI chose differently each time.

Two kinds of people see this

✕ Without fundamentals

  • "AI said it, must be right."
  • Ships the answer 200 to the manager.
  • Never notices the definition switched.
  • Looks careless when it's caught.

✓ With fundamentals

  • "Wait — earlier the middle was 25, now it's 20?"
  • Spots the inconsistency in seconds.
  • Picks the right definition for this problem.
  • Looks sharp and trustworthy.
AI can give you an answer. But YOU decide whether it's the right answer.
Fundamentals don't make you obsolete — they make you the person who catches what AI misses.
