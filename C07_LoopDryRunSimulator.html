The pattern: when a loop starts at start, runs while i <= end, and updates by i += stride, the number of iterations is roughly (end − start) / stride + 1. For Quiz 1 (i=1, stride=1, end=20) that's 20. For Quiz 2 (stride=2, end=n) that's n/2. For Quiz 3 (stride=3) that's n/3. Bigger stride → fewer iterations.
iters ≈ range / stride
