WHY THIS WORKS
Reading source row by row and writing destination column by column achieves the transpose. Source row 0 becomes destination col 0. Source row 1 becomes destination col 1. And so on.
Watch the dimensions → a 3×4 source becomes a 4×3 result. The diagonal stays in place; everything else flips around it.
Mental shortcut → "Row of source becomes column of destination."
