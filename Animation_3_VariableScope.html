Variable scope — same name, different worlds

When a function takes parameters named a, b and the caller has its own a, b — these are DIFFERENT variables. They live in separate scopes. Changing one doesn't change the other. This is the most-missed concept in the Quiz section.

Notice → main has its own a and the function has its own a. They share a name. They do NOT share a value. The function sees only what was passed in.
