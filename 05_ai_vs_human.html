AI vs YOU

Both are powerful — but at different things. The future belongs to people who know which is which.

🤖
What AI is great at
Fast, tireless, knows a lot
✓ Writing boilerplate — repetitive code patterns it has seen a million times.
✓ Explaining syntax — what a semicolon does, what a loop is.
✓ Suggesting fixes when you describe a bug clearly.
✓ Speed — produces 100 lines in seconds. Doesn't get tired.
VS
🧠
What YOU are great at
Judgment, taste, ownership
✓ Understanding the problem — what does the user actually need?
✓ Breaking it into steps — the messy, creative part AI struggles with.
✓ Spotting when AI is wrong — and it is wrong, often.
✓ Owning the result — your name is on it. AI can't be accountable.
Companies aren't replacing developers with AI. They're hiring developers who use AI well.
Developer + AI  >  AI alone  >  Developer alone
